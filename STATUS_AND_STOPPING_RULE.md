# Research Status and Stopping Rule

## What is finished enough to study now

The public portfolio already contains rerunnable work across all three stacks:

- AI internals: audit loops, evidence conflict, state transport, probes, and
  bounded component edits.
- Science: numerical-reasoning audits and dose-response identifiability.
- Security/formal systems: proof-carrying policies, interrupt routing, and
  lattice consistency.
- Shared substrate: EML audit, Unified Address Space, and answer-packet
  integrity.

Each finished public project should have a README, frozen fixture or protocol,
saved result, stated boundary, and a rerun command or verifier. The Research
Atlas is the reading order.

## UAS status

UAS has three complementary results:

1. A deterministic type-confusion retrieval control: typed identity prevents
   the controlled wrong-type retrieval failure.
2. A local-model behavior test that tied: no behavioral gain was confirmed on
   the first easy 24-case corpus.
3. A metadata-loss ablation: when the flat baseline removes the needed type,
   retaining that type in UAS changes local Qwen3 4B selection from 8/24 to
   24/24. This is a narrow information-preservation result.

That is enough to make the UAS idea legible and honest. It is not enough to
claim a general AI architecture breakthrough.

## Stop rule for this research pass

Stop generating new synthetic experiments after the current artifacts are
verified, visualized, and documented. Do not run another variation merely to
improve a percentage. Start a new UAS experiment only when it has all of these:

1. An independently authored or externally sourced, permitted corpus;
2. a frozen protocol and answer key before model outputs are inspected;
3. a matched flat baseline that does not erase information accidentally;
4. at least one independent model or grader;
5. a stated negative-result plan and publication destination.

## Next worthwhile work, later

- Ask a domain instructor or peer to author blind science and safe-security
  records without seeing the retrieval code.
- Run flat versus UAS retrieval on that fixed corpus with two local models.
- Grade answer correctness, cited-address validity, abstention, and
  wrong-domain contamination separately.

Until those inputs exist, the right move is to study, explain, and improve the
existing evidence—not manufacture more synthetic wins.
