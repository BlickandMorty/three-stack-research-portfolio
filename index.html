One program, three stacks

The domains change. The discipline does not.

Inside a model, evidence can conflict. In an assay, measurements can be contaminated or structurally incomplete. At a security boundary, later instructions can claim authority they do not have. I use deterministic oracles, interventions, receipts, and held-out tests to separate a persuasive story from evidence that survives contact with reality.

Scientific Reasoning Audit Loops ↗

The original audit found 61.1%→79.2% self-audit improvement with three regressions. A public-precommitted, exactly label-balanced replication found 62.5% direct, 75.0% intrinsic, and 81.9% verifier-gated (14 repairs, 0 regressions).

Boundary: oracle feedback protects direct-correct cases by design; this is inference-time scaffolding, not learned science.

AI systems × science × security90 frozen type-confusion queries

Unified Address Space Reasoning Lab ↗

An independent UAS retrieval experiment gives science observations, safe defensive-security traces, and AI-evaluation notes stable typed addresses. Flat ranking returned the wrong type on 90/90 synthetic type-confusion queries; typed retrieval returned the target on 90/90. A local Qwen3 4B type-erasure ablation then went 8/24 flat versus 24/24 typed UAS, with wrong-domain answers 16→0.

Boundary: the type label is available to UAS by design. A separate 24-case local-model follow-up tied at 24/24 in both conditions, so it did not confirm a general behavioral gain. This is not factual truth or live model routing.

Provenance × AI systems90 valid + 540 tamper cases

Answer-Packet Integrity Lab ↗

A deterministic answer-packet validator accepts 90 frozen science, safe-security, and AI-evaluation packets, while rejecting six specific metadata corruptions across 540 deliberate tamper cases: schema, identifier, fallback acknowledgement, label, evidence link, and replay digest.

Boundary: packet integrity is not factual truth, model provenance, or deployed-system security. It is a narrow check that a declared answer interface did not silently lose its linked evidence and replay material.

AI internalsCausal intervention

Evidence-Conflict Circuits ↗

A 256-case four-valued evidence task, 48-pair bidirectional residual patch, and an eight-feature sparse intervention that failed to reproduce the whole-state effect. A later frozen 32-packet science/security prompt test found that an internal evidence ledger did not improve Qwen3 1.7B over direct classification: both scored 15/32.

Boundary: a causal hidden-state result is not automatically a sparse circuit, and a plausible prompt explanation is not an improvement until it beats a frozen test.

AI internals × memory30 matched direct-state pairs

Compression-Control Reasoning Lab ↗

A direct Qwen3 0.6B Transformer audit measured final residual-stream geometry without answer generation: deleting a decisive assignment fact produced lower similarity to the matched full-context state than deleting an irrelevant fact (0.990804 versus 0.998421; precommitted gap +0.007617). A first 18-case hook study repaired geometry but failed its clean-state gate. An independent 48-case replication then chose scale on development cases only and, on 24 held-out cases, gained +0.002557 over baseline, beat random by +0.002673, and kept clean drop at 0.000441.

Boundary: this is a narrow latent-geometry control result, not recovered memory, answer improvement, a permanent parameter update, safe general self-correction, or evidence of emotion/valence.

Scientific Evidence-State Transport ↗

The frozen layer-20 patch kept its sign on 31/36 short numeric science pairs, then failed a separately preregistered 30-pair longer-chain replication.

Boundary: a longer-chain replication failed, and its post-hoc layout explanation reversed on 24 untouched bases—rejecting robust, layer-specific, or confirmed interaction claims.

AI internals × science120 format-heldout cases

Representation–Causality Gap Audit ↗

A source-only layer-20 probe fell from 100% entity-CV to 50% on Quill. A fixed multiformat probe trained on source plus Quill then reached 79.2% on the predeclared third Mosaic format; the model head reached 49.2%.

Boundary: this is a linear readout result, not model training, causal control, or scientific proof; REFUTED remained only 50% accurate.

AI internals × safety87 held-out prompts

Component-Edit Bound Audit ↗

A frozen transcoder-hook locality envelope passed with 2/87 prompt violations and no eligible PPL drift above 1.0; rejected development fits and random controls remain in the record.

Boundary: an empirical hook-edit result is not a universal parameter-edit theorem or a semantic circuit.

Science × provenanceprecommitted control-bias sweep

Dose-Response Audit Lab ↗

Monte Carlo experiments separate outlier robustness from structural identifiability. A 400-curve-per-condition control-bias sweep found that fixed-control error increases IC50 risk monotonically; at 40 response units, about 54% of fits were at least twofold wrong.

Boundary: this is a synthetic control-bias map. A SHA-256 receipt detects changed result material, not whether a laboratory control was biologically correct.

Security96 + 48 + 72 passive cases

Proof-Carrying Policy Evals ↗

A deterministic ALLOW/DENY/ESCALATE oracle with canonical receipts tests surface robustness. A public-precommitted 72-case certificate confirmation improved accuracy 40.3%→59.7% but raised unauthorized ALLOWs 3→6.

Boundary: it failed the precommitted safety rule. Aggregate accuracy can improve while the dangerous error profile worsens.

Formal systemsExhaustive finite audit

Lattice State Consistency Lab ↗

The product state space passes lattice laws and 2,592 migration checks. A frozen 84-rule atlas then found that all-required-item rules preserve both operations; multi-item any-of is join-only, at-most-one is meet-only, and exactly-one is neither.

Boundary: this is an exact finite policy grammar result, not a theorem about arbitrary production authorization rules.

Sheaf Connectome Sanity Lab ↗

The H0 identity passed, but my canon's positive spectral-gap/modularity criterion reversed to ρ = −0.972; label shuffles and degree-preserving rewires collapsed near zero.

Boundary: a planted synthetic falsifier is not transformer, biological, or production-network validation.

AI × science × security108 held-out prompts

Interrupt Router Calibration Lab ↗

A frozen logit-margin fallback reduced unauthorized ALLOWs from 4 to 1 at a 24.1% interrupt rate while accuracy stayed at 38.9%.

Boundary: always-ESCALATE tied accuracy, the base classifier remained weak, and this is an output router—not an attention-kernel intervention.
